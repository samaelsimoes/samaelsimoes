## 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience** designing, developing, integrating, and modernizing enterprise applications. My work spans **banking, industrial operations, healthcare, and logistics**, with a strong focus on maintainability, performance, security, and business impact.

My core expertise is in the **Java / Spring Boot ecosystem** and modern front-end development with **Angular, React, and TypeScript**. I work across the software lifecycle—from understanding complex requirements and defining architecture to implementation, testing, integration, and delivery.

I'm also the **Founder & CEO of [SPS Digital Solutions Technology](https://spstechnology.com.br/)**, a software company focused on custom development, systems integration, technology consulting, and engineering outsourcing.

- 🏗️ **Engineering:** Clean Architecture, SOLID, domain modeling, REST APIs, microservices, and distributed systems
- 🔄 **Modernization:** Legacy application evolution, incremental refactoring, and enterprise integrations
- ☁️ **Cloud & Delivery:** AWS, Docker, CI/CD, and infrastructure automation
- 🔐 **Quality & Security:** Authentication, authorization, automated testing, and maintainable code
- 🤝 **Collaboration:** Technical mentoring, cross-functional delivery, and translating business needs into engineering decisions
- 🎓 **Education:** Degrees in Software Engineering and Systems Analysis & Development
- 🌎 **Location:** Brazil | Interested in international engineering collaboration and opportunities
